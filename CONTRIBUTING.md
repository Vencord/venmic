# Contributing to Venmic 

Venmic builds upon the [contributing guidelines of Vencord](https://github.com/Vendicated/Vencord/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
Please pay special attention to the **AI Policy**.

## AI Policy

**On top of the Vencord AI-Policy**, venmic has additional rules:

- **AI written code is forbidden**.

- **Don't post AI generated texts in issues, discussions or PRs**. 
  AI is very good at being overly verbose and including noise that distracts from the main point. 
  You can use AI to assist you, but you must trim it down and ensure it is concise and relevant.
  We also expect you to investigate issues yourself, or at least thoroughly verify that whatever an AI tells you is actually the case.
